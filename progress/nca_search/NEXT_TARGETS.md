# Next NCA targets for transformer transfer

This ranks **hypotheses**, not observed language-transfer correlations. Saved-world features have no per-world English outcome. The only promising transfer result so far is the selected **high horizontal patch-copy** corpus: five of five model seeds win at 1M TinyStories tokens, and four of five win at 10M; mean 10M paired loss gain is 0.01864 with an exploratory interval crossing zero. The low-copy directional control loses at 1M in all five model seeds. All of these use one candidate corpus with roughly half its rules shared with the reference. See [experiment log](EXPERIMENT_LOG.md) and [corpus audit](ITERATION_04_AUDIT.md).

The following order favors a dependency the *actual* 256-token causal patch stream can see, variation within the 40–50 gzip band, and a selector that can be sampled without changing the data contract. `ρ` values below are world-level Spearman associations in existing deterministic saved-world screens. They measure feature overlap, **not** transfer prediction. A small `ρ` does not make a selector causal or eliminate unmeasured rule differences.

## Focused screen update

Subsequent read-only screens sharpen the choice of a new selector. [Cell-transition information](TRANSITION_INFO_TARGET.md) corresponds to **0.5845 bits/patch** mean held-out predictive gain from a world-fitted factorized transition table on causal visible targets, with weak association to gzip and horizontal patch-copy excess. This is the strongest direct patch-prediction diagnostic here, but the transformer is not given that world-specific table. It merits a controlled high/low probe after the existing high-copy result is replicated on fresh rule corpora. [Vertical lag-6](VERTICAL_LAG_TARGET.md) remains a distinct local-copy target: its sampled association with horizontal excess is only `ρ=+.086`, and the completed high/low-horizontal cohorts have nearly equal sampled vertical excess. Its reported median uses 1,000 reference worlds, so freeze a full-reference cutoff before sampling. [Visible lag-76](TEMPORAL_TARGET.md) has an exact 25,000-world median, but selecting its high half also moves lag-38, horizontal copy, and cell change. [Late entropy stability](ENTROPY_STABILITY_TARGET.md) stays secondary: the high-copy corpus has *more* late entropy decline despite better downstream 1M loss. None of these screens measures a new English-learning outcome.

## Decision rule

Test one selector at a time against the same unfiltered 40–50 reference, exact gzip sub-bin quotas, world and label counts, tokenizer, model seeds, and TinyStories schedule. A seed-0 1M result is triage only. The high-copy result was found after several exploratory selectors and its five model seeds share one selected corpus; unadjusted intervals should not be interpreted as confirmatory significance. Prioritize an **independent rule-corpus replication** of high-copy alongside any new winner, then paired model seeds and the full 10M curve. Track both endpoint loss and full-curve AUC because the high-copy benefit peaked around 1–3M and narrowed by 10M.

Changing generator parameters, such as identity bias, temperature, or trajectories per rule, remains a separate policy experiment; [rule-design notes](RULE_DESIGN.md) explain why those interventions shift multiple world features and need their own controls. Avoid prioritizing lag-38 exact copy, patch entropy, context repeat, or raw bigram repeat as first follow-ups: they largely restate cell-change or gzip in the current data.
